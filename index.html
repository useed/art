// 01 — WHO AM I

One man. Every frame.
A complete story.

I'm Youssef Mostafa — "Useed" — a video editor and content creator with over 5 years of experience in content creation and visual production. I transform raw footage and ideas into professional visual content that reaches the audience and achieves the goal.

I work as a One-Man Crew — a full production team on my own, from content writing and planning, to shooting and directing, to editing and sound design, all the way to final delivery. I also have a solid background in digital marketing, so I don't just care that content looks good — I make sure it fits the audience and serves its purpose.

Alongside my work with clients and companies, I'm also a content creator who produces and releases my own content on YouTube, Instagram & TikTok. That gives me hands-on understanding of what grabs people's attention — and how to turn a simple idea into strong, shareable, engaging content.

I speak both Egyptian and Gulf (Khaleeji) Arabic fluently — I lived 17 years in Saudi Arabia (Yanbu's Royal Commission).

My goal is simple: to deliver professional content that combines quality and creativity — and always exceeds expectations.
